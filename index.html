Creative Profile

I design products that honor human attention and solve genuine operational friction.

I am Janette Amoatemaa Sarfo — a UI/UX designer and Computer Science student. My practice centers on the intersection of human cognitive patterns, systemic information architecture, and real-world utility.

How I Think & Build

The Method: From ambiguity to intentional clarity.

Great UX is not decorated at the end; it is synthesized through rigorous inquiry, systems mapping, and iterative validation.

01

Inquiry & Immersion

I interview real stakeholders, observe behavioral bottlenecks in their native environment, and map the emotional friction points before generating solutions.

  • Contextual stakeholder interviews
  • Cognitive load & anxiety mapping
  • Competitive landscape heuristics
02

Information Architecture

Structuring data models, navigational hierarchies, and user journeys. My computer science background ensures data flow aligns cleanly with interface logic.

  • Low-fidelity wireframing & user flows
  • Entity relationship & state diagrams
  • Error state & edge case anticipation
03

Systemic UI & Tokens

Building purposeful design systems with distinct domain personalities. Establishing typography scales, accessible color ratios, and component variables.

  • Auto-layout design systems in Figma
  • WCAG 2.1 AA contrast compliance
  • Micro-interaction physics & motion states
04

Validation & Code Hand-off

Testing interactive prototypes with users and producing structured developer hand-off specs that minimize implementation ambiguities.

  • Interactive prototype usability tests
  • Design-to-code token alignment
  • Post-release user feedback synthesis
